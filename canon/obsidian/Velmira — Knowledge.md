# Velmira — Knowledge

This note tracks what **Velmira can legitimately know**, separated by subject and source. It is not a list of GM truth.

Velmira is **Carla's active player character**. Her established knowledge remains canon. She does not automatically gain new knowledge from events she did not witness; Carla determines her actions, investigations and what she chooses to share.

## Firsthand — Kestrel Return / Session Three aftermath

Velmira personally knows that:

- the caravan was sent onto the wrong lower line after a route marker proved wrong;
- the groundfall happened before the cacklemaw attack;
- [[Joric Noll]] survived and returned with the others;
- the Stone-Lip Hollow cacklemaw attack was resolved;
- the surviving caravan members returned to [[Greywake]];
- [[Ash-Plate]] returned alive but injured;
- [[Lowbell]] returned serviceable;
- [[Odie]] opened the heavy pale transport case at Stone-Lip Hollow;
- the case contained a large intact precision-made ceramic waterworks plate;
- [[Rennic Vale]] believed the plate could matter to Greywake's waterworks;
- the case was resealed, kept flat and reached Greywake intact;
- the recovered Plate is now in Greywake under examination.

Velmira does **not** automatically know the Plate's exact function, compatibility, manufacture history, whether it will fit, or what final benefit it may provide.

## Social knowledge

**Tier 2, with higher detail only when earned through a specific relationship or investigation.**

**Source:** trade, relationships, observation, conversation.

Velmira is especially good at reading:

- fear;
- need;
- social pressure;
- rumours;
- who is talking to whom;
- when people are avoiding a subject or protecting someone.

This lets her recognise patterns and likely motives. It does not make rumours true.

## The Faithful

**Tier 2 public / lived familiarity.**

**Source:** local life, social contact, current family connections.

Velmira can reasonably know the Faithful's public rituals, language, gathering habits, visible personalities and social influence.

She does not automatically know private interpretations, internal disagreements or hidden conclusions unless someone tells her or she investigates them.

## Nemi, Tavi and Lysa

**Personal knowledge.**

Velmira knows [[Nemi]] personally and is directly connected to Nemi's current Stilling case.

She knows [[Tavi]] and [[Lysa]] through her own family and relationships.

Exact hidden truths about the Stilling remain outside Velmira's knowledge unless discovered in play.

The cause and cure of the Stilling remain unresolved. Velmira can pursue leads and make consequential choices through Carla's play, not through assumed offscreen discoveries.

## The thing that followed her

Velmira remembers surviving a desert journey while an unidentified presence followed her for much of the day without attacking.

She asked [[Clay]] to watch for signs of it.

What the presence was remains unknown.

## Odie's Oldwork discoveries

**Private trusted knowledge.**

**Source:** Odie.

Velmira knows about:

- Odie's Oldwork finger;
- the clean white tunnel;
- the [[White Door]].

Their true nature remains undefined.

This knowledge is private and should not automatically become shared party knowledge.

## Knowledge boundary

Velmira can be very good at identifying **social patterns, testimony, fear and contradiction** without automatically knowing whether an interpretation is objectively true.

As an active PC, she retains what she already knows. New information must be earned through play or established communication; her social skills do not provide automatic access to hidden truths.
