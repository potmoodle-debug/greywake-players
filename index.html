Session Five recap

From Greywake to the Stripped Yards

After a day of investigation and preparation in Greywake, Marek, Odie and Velmira set out together to search for Thirst-Marrow for Meren. They reached the Stripped Yards south of town. They found animal tracks leading into abandoned houses, then heard stone strike twice among the buildings to the west before everything fell quiet.

The group had not collected any Thirst-Marrow by the end of the session. The sound’s source remains unknown.
