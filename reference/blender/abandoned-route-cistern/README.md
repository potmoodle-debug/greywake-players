## Reconstruction rules
Treat the environment images as different views of ONE coherent location, not alternate designs.
Preserve major architectural anchors and relative spatial logic first.
Make the 360 references agree spatially.
Infer unseen joins conservatively.
Keep construction practical, aged, repairable, and grounded in Greywake's desert setting.
Avoid modern or overt sci-fi visual language.

## Visual target
Realistic, cinematic, grounded desert fantasy; believable scale; naturalistic wear; plausible stone, mineral staining, dust, aged fittings and long abandonment.

## Canon caution
The current Cistern Plate is Oldwork. The older plate image may reflect an earlier canon state. Do not use it to redefine current lore or manufacturing origin.

## Blender deliverable goal
Create one navigable coherent 3D environment suitable for cinematic establishing shots, player-height views, top-down tactical views, and possible later VR/360 exploration.
